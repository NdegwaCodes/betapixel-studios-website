Applying a UV DTF transfer takes about five minutes and no special tools. Most problems (edges lifting, a letter staying on the film, bubbles under the design) come from the surface or the speed of the final peel, not the transfer. This guide covers both the steps and the fixes.

## What You Need

- The transfer, still on its backing
- Isopropyl alcohol (IPA) and a lint-free cloth, or clean water and a dry cloth
- A plastic squeegee or an old bank card
- Masking tape (optional, for positioning)

## Step 1: Prepare the Surface

This is the step that decides whether the transfer lasts.

- **Clean** with isopropyl alcohol. It removes grease and fingerprints and evaporates quickly. Water works if IPA is not available, but the surface must then dry completely.
- **Remove factory coatings** where you can. Some new bottles and tumblers have a thin protective film or oily residue from manufacturing.
- **Let it reach room temperature.** A bottle straight from a cold car or fridge will gather condensation, and the adhesive will not grip moisture.
- **Do not touch** the area once cleaned.

## Step 2: Position Before You Peel

Hold the transfer against the item with the backing still on. For straight placement, stick a strip of masking tape across the top of the transfer onto the item to act as a hinge. On bottles, check the position from the front at eye level — a design that looks centred from above is often off-centre from the front.

## Step 3: Peel the Backing

Peel the backing sheet away at a low angle, keeping the design on the clear top film. The adhesive side is now exposed. Do not let it touch your fingers or fold onto itself.

## Step 4: Lay Down from the Centre Out

Lower the transfer onto the surface and press the centre first, then smooth outwards to the edges. This pushes air out instead of trapping it. On a curved bottle, work from the centre line around the curve, not from one edge to the other.

## Step 5: Rub Down Firmly

Rub the whole design with a squeegee or card for 20 to 30 seconds using firm pressure. Pay extra attention to:

- small details and thin lines
- the edges of each letter
- any area over a curve or seam

The adhesive is pressure-activated: the harder and more evenly you rub, the better the bond.

## Step 6: Peel the Top Film Slowly

Peel the clear film away **slowly** at a sharp angle, almost folding it back on itself. Watch the design as you go. If any part starts to lift with the film, stop, lay the film back down, rub that area again, and continue.

Peeling quickly or at a shallow angle is the most common cause of pieces staying on the film.

## Step 7: Leave It for 24 Hours

The adhesive reaches full strength after about a day. Avoid washing, soaking or heavy handling in that time.

## Applying to Curved Items

Bottles, tumblers and mugs curve in one direction and can take a transfer easily. Items that curve in two directions at once, such as a round ball or the shoulder of a bottle, will wrinkle a large transfer. On these:

- keep the design narrower, about a third of the item's circumference at most;
- split a wide design into separate pieces;
- or place it on the straightest part of the item.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Part of the design stays on the film | Film peeled too fast or rubbed too lightly | Lay it back, rub again, peel slower at a sharper angle |
| Edges lift after a few days | Grease on the surface, or a non-stick coating | Clean with IPA; check the surface is not silicone or waxed |
| Bubbles under the design | Laid down from one edge, trapping air | Press bubbles to the nearest edge; prick large ones with a pin and press flat |
| Wrinkles on a curved item | Design too wide for the curve | Use a narrower design or split it |
| Transfer does not stick at all | Silicone, wax, or very textured surface | Unsuitable surface; see the list below |

## Surfaces That Will Not Hold a Transfer

Silicone, wax-coated finishes, heavily textured stone and fabric. For garments, use DTF; see [t-shirt printing](/custom-apparel/). If you are not sure about a surface, send us a photo before ordering.

## Care After Application

- Hand wash with mild detergent.
- Avoid dishwashers, microwaves and sustained heat above about 60°C.
- Do not scrub the design with abrasive pads.
